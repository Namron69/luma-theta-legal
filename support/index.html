Support

Luma Theta for iPhone · Operator: Vladislav

This page is the App Store Support URL for Luma Theta. The FAQ below covers restore, refunds, what’s free versus Lifetime Access, and safety.

For product questions, use the Support link on the App Store product page, or write through the App Store Connect support channel once the app is published. Until a branded inbox exists, purchase and billing help is handled by Apple (see Refunds). We do not require an account; if you email after we publish a contact address, include your Apple ID purchase receipt date so we can help.

Restore Lifetime Access

Open Settings → Restore Purchase (or tap Restore Purchase on the Lifetime Access screen) while signed in with the Apple ID you used to buy. There is no Luma Theta account; your purchase belongs to your Apple ID.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

What's free and what's included with Lifetime Access

Theta Studio, all breathing practices, the Learn section, and the written Progressive Relaxation technique are free. The guided Progressive Relaxation sequence offers a free preview (preparation and the first area). Lifetime Access ($9.99 in the US, one time, no subscription) unlocks the full guided sequence and three journeys of 7, 14, and 21 days.

Binaural sound tips

  • Use stereo headphones — binaural beats need separate left and right channels. On speakers or AirPlay the app plays an ambient preview instead.
  • Keep the volume low and comfortable.
  • Don't listen while driving or doing anything that needs your full attention.

Safety

Luma Theta is a wellness tool, not medical care. Stop any exercise that causes pain, dizziness, or breathlessness. If you have epilepsy, a heart condition, or other health concerns, talk to a qualified professional first.
