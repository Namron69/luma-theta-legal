Privacy Policy

Luma Theta for iPhone · Effective date: October 2026 · Operator: Vladislav

Luma Theta is a wellness app for binaural theta sound, breathing practice, and progressive relaxation. It is designed to work without collecting personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. Luma Theta has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or usage data, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

What stays on your device

The app stores a few preferences locally on your iPhone using Apple's standard settings storage (UserDefaults), for example:

  • whether you have finished onboarding,
  • whether haptic feedback is turned on,
  • which day you have reached in each guided journey.

This information never leaves your device through Luma Theta. It is removed when you delete the app, and may be included in your own iCloud or device backups under Apple's terms.

Audio

All sound is generated on the device in real time. Luma Theta does not use the microphone and does not record or upload audio.

Purchases

Lifetime Access is an optional one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We never see your payment details. The app asks StoreKit only whether Lifetime Access is active for your Apple ID so it can unlock content and restore purchases. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children

Luma Theta does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Health disclaimer

Luma Theta is an educational wellness tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
